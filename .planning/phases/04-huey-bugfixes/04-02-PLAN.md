Fix docker-compose.yml volume configuration to remove conflicting named volume.

<context>
@C:/dev/INDT/InsuranceManager/docker-compose.yml

Current docker-compose.yml has both:
1. Bind mount in services: `./huey_data:/app/huey_data` (lines 20, 37)
2. Named volume at bottom: `huey_data:` (line 44-45)

This creates a conflict - the named volume shadows the bind mount. Per D-02, both containers use same path `/app/huey_data` with bind mount only.
